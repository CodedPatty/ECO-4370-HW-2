Interpretation

The estimates show positive returns to education for all four subgroups. The education coefficient is about 0.0776 for men with high school or less, 0.1171 for men with College+, 0.0731 for women with high school or less, and 0.1157 for women with College+. Since the dependent variable is log hourly wage, an additional year of schooling is associated with a larger percentage increase in hourly wages for the College+ groups.

The experience coefficient is positive and squared experience is negative in every subgroup, producing the standard concave Mincer profile. The model explains more wage variation in the College+ groups (R-squared about 0.185–0.186) than in the High School or Less groups (about 0.092–0.121).
